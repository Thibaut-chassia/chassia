Data protection

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Version 2.0
Visitor, not signed in

Nothing is sent to a server. Your preferences and usage counter stay on your device.

Member, signed in

Your logbook, safe and photos are hosted on Supabase, in the European Union — so they're accessible from all your devices.

Member access is obtained with a personal code, valid for one year.

ChassIA is a hunting assistance application. Full access is granted individually, using a personal code. This page explains precisely what information is processed, how, and what your rights are.

01

Who is responsible for your data

ChassIA is a personal project, developed and managed privately for use within a limited circle of hunters. The data controller can be reached at:

Contact: contact@chassia.be

02

The data ChassIA processes

What is recorded depends on how you use the application.

Visitor, not signed in

Your information stays locally, in your browser's storage (localStorage). It is neither transmitted nor remotely accessible.

Preferences

Your display language and usage settings.

Usage counter

The number of AI questions asked today, to manage the daily limit. No question content is kept, only a number.

Member, signed in

Member access requires signing in with a Google account and a personal code. Once signed in, your data is hosted on Supabase rather than on your device, so it's accessible from all your devices.

Email address

Provided by your Google account, to identify your access.

Hunting logbook

Harvests, dates, species, calibers, locations, notes, and up to 3 photos per entry.

Safe

The documents you deposit there (hunting licence, insurance, certificates…) and your equipment list.

AI credits counter

The number of AI questions used this month, to manage your monthly quota.

Nothing is collected beyond what you add yourself in the application.

03

Data sent to the artificial intelligence

When you use the AI assistant or request caliber or ammunition advice, the text of your request is sent to an AI service to generate a response.

Who processes these requests

Requests are processed by Anthropic (Claude models), via a secure connection. Anthropic acts as a technical processor to produce the response. ChassIA does not retain a history of your conversations: each question is handled independently.

Our recommendation

Avoid entering sensitive information or information identifying others in your AI questions. A question about a caliber or a regulation doesn't need to contain personal data.

04

Hosting

The application is hosted by Vercel Inc. (340 Pine Street, Suite 701, San Francisco, CA 94104, United States). Standard technical logs of a web server (such as the connection IP address) may be processed for operation and security purposes.

Members' data (logbook, safe, photos, documents) is hosted by Supabase, on servers located in Frankfurt, Germany — within the European Union.

05

Cookies and audience measurement

ChassIA uses no advertising cookies nor any third-party trackers: no Google Analytics, no social media pixel, no profiling.

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to measure traffic: an anonymous measurement, without cookies, that does not allow you to be individually identified.

06

Legal basis and purposes

To the extent that data is processed, the legal bases (within the meaning of Article 6 GDPR) are:

  • Performance of the service, within the scope of the individual access you requested and were granted as a Member.
  • Your consent, when you voluntarily send a question to the AI assistant.
  • Legitimate interest in the proper operation and security of the application.

The purposes are limited to: operating the application, managing your Membership, answering your AI requests. No data is used for marketing, advertising, or resold.

07

Retention period

  • Visitor data (preferences, counter): kept on your device for as long as you wish, deleted by clearing your browser data.
  • Member data: kept on Supabase for the duration of your access (one year from the activation of your code) and as long as you remain a member. You may request its deletion at any time.
  • AI requests: sent for the time needed to produce the response, without retention of their content by ChassIA.
08

Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability regarding your personal data.

As a Visitor, you access, modify or delete your data directly, by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

As a Member, since your data is hosted on Supabase, write to contact@chassia.be to access it, have it corrected, or deleted. We respond as promptly as possible.

09

Transfers outside the European Union

Two technical providers are established in the United States:

  • Vercel, for hosting the application.
  • Anthropic, for processing requests sent to the AI assistant.

Using these services may involve a transfer of data to the United States, framed by the appropriate safeguards provided for by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses and equivalent mechanisms). Supabase, which hosts Members' data, is based in the European Union and is therefore not concerned by such a transfer.

10

Security

The application is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Requests to the AI pass through a secure gateway that exposes no access key on the browser side. Members' data is encrypted in transit to Supabase and stored in a space accessible only via your authenticated account.

11

Becoming and remaining a member

Member access is obtained only via a personal, single-use code, handed out individually. This code activates your access for a period of one year from activation.

After this period, your Member access expires automatically. Your data remains stored; contact us if you wish to retrieve it, have it deleted, or renew your access.

12

Minors

ChassIA is aimed at licensed hunters, therefore at adults. The application is not intended for minors and does not knowingly collect their data.

13

Changes to this policy

This policy may evolve, in particular when new features are added. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page. In the event of a significant change in the processing of your data, the information will be made visible in the application.

14

Complaint

If you believe that the processing of your data does not comply with the regulations, you may lodge a complaint with the Belgian supervisory authority:
